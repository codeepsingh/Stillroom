# Typography

Stillroom bundles Fraunces (display) and Manrope (body) from the pinned `@fontsource/fraunces` and `@fontsource/manrope` packages. Both are distributed under the SIL Open Font License; license copies are included here. All active font requests are served locally.

The TTF files and `source.css` are Google Fonts reference assets. The application's active stylesheet imports Fontsource rather than `source.css`.
